Two questions that might be the same question:
- **Am I making money from this?** (the share frame — one company, internal goodness)
- **Am I leaving things better than the last guy?** (the ripple frame — what moved because you were here, including around corners you can't see)

## The problem

- Under capitalism, investment is singular. One share. One company.
- The "goodness" of that share is measured **inside** that company — revenue, margin, moat, etc.
- That works when value creation and value capture happen in the same place.
- Doesn't work so well when what you're building is a **capability** other people pick up and run with.

## Rippling around corners

- First-order effects are obvious. Image company → people make images. Steel gets cheap → people buy steel.
- Second/third order you can sometimes still draw on a whiteboard (steel → rails → trade → factories).
- **The interesting bit is what ripples around corners** — effects you can't see from where you're standing.
- Categories you didn't file under yours. Markets that didn't have names yet.
- Shares measure the stone. Ripples measure what moved — including around corners you didn't know existed.
- "Leaving it better" might mean the corners, not the splash.

## Midjourney → MRI

- Five years ago: image company. Diffusion. AI art. Cat pictures.
- June 2026: **Midjourney Medical** — full-body ultrasound scanner, ~60 sec, claiming MRI-comparable quality in many cases.[1]
- Image company. Now MRI machine.
- Nobody pricing "AI art platform" had medical hardware on the map. The corner was there.
- Same skill (noisy signal → reconstructed image) — bodies, not prompts.

## Steel / industrial revolution (the old version of this)

- Bessemer → cheap steel. First ripple obvious.
- Second ripples still visible: rails, bridges, skyscrapers.
- Corners nobody filed under "steel":
  - Canning / food at distance
  - Machine tools cutting machine tools (factories building factories)
  - Shipping at scale that wasn't rational before
- Steelmaker's share captured ripple 1, maybe 2. The world-changing bit was everything else.
- Did the steel guys "leave it better"? Some did, some didn't — but the pond moved either way.

## AI — same shape?

- Ripple 1 (priced in?): chatbots, code assist, image gen, summarisation
- Ripple 2 (starting?): niche tools that weren't worth building, solo founders doing team work, small biz analysis
- Ripple 3 (the corners?): ???
  - Cheap reasoning — specialist thinking in random fields?
  - Cheap code — workflows "too small to automate" now worth building?
  - Cheap translation — not just language, domain translation?
  - Cheap design — prototype cost drops, way more ideas get tested?

## Why the share frame breaks

- Investor questions: moat, margin, capture. All about the **company**.
- Cheap steel value mostly didn't stay with steel companies.
- AI value probably won't mostly stay with "AI companies" either.
- Bookkeeping firm 2x clients. Regional manufacturer with weekend-built ERP. Image company → scanner.
- None of that on OpenAI's balance sheet. All ripples around corners.
- You can make money from this *and* leave it better — but they're not the same metric.

## Questions / things to figure out

- Is there a better unit of measure than share price for this? (GDP? productivity? new submarkets? idk)
- "Am I making money from this" vs "did I leave it better than the last guy" — when do those diverge? When do they align?
- What does "leave it better" even mean if most of the goodness ripples around a corner you'll never see?
- What corners am I missing?
